# How it started

*Our story.* This page is also in the app at `/how-it-started.html`.

CureBranch is a free, open-source project. One person started it after losing his dad to cancer. He kept asking one question: what if patients could see what worked for people whose cancer looks like theirs?

> In honor of my dad, whose son wishes AI had come a few months sooner. Let tomorrow be decided by patients and their own medical records. Here's to you, Dad.

**[Start the easy setup guide](https://curebranch.ai/?setup=1)**: anyone can sign up in a few minutes. No account, no password. Your records stay with you.

## The idea, in plain words

1. **Your records come from official places.** You connect your hospital portal, like MyChart. Records that come straight from a hospital or lab are marked *verified*.
2. **Your records stay private, on your device.** They are saved only on your own phone or computer. CureBranch has no server that keeps your records.
3. **You pick how much to share: 4 levels.** 1) Private: only on your device. 2) Basic: ranges like Stage 3 and age 60 to 69. 3) Detailed: adds exact age and dates. 4) Full: adds your city and full treatment details. Nothing is picked for you. Never your name, birthday, street address or record numbers. Just a random code like k7x2m9. You can move to a lower level any time; that stops sharing from then on, but what was already shared stays out there.
4. **AI finds people like you.** Your own AI, like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Grok, can compare your branch with others. It shows what worked for people whose cancer looks most like yours. You bring that to your doctors.
5. **The tree grows.** Every shared summary is a branch. Branches split where cancers are different and join where they are alike. The more people join, the more everyone can learn.

## What "open source" means

- Anyone can see the code that runs CureBranch.
- Anyone can copy it and make it better, for free.
- No company owns it. Nobody can lock it up or sell your data.
- No money is involved. No fees, no ads, no donations.

## How it works, step by step

1. Press **Start the easy setup guide**.
2. Watch a short video. Check "I watched and understand".
3. Get your private code. No account. No password. No email.
4. Connect your hospital portal, or skip it for now.
5. Choose private or public. Private is how it starts.
6. Choose what happens after you're gone, and name a person you trust.
7. If you want, set up your own AI. Then you're done. You can change anything later in Settings.

Most people finish in a few minutes. A Listen button reads every step out loud.
