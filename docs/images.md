# Images

## Today
- **No stock photos are used or downloaded.** The app uses an icon drawn from the tree mark (`app/icons/`) and, on profile pages, an abstract branch pattern generated from each random code, so every anonymous profile looks different without using faces or template avatars.
- A patient's own photo appears only if they opt in. It is re-encoded on their device at 512 px max, which removes location and camera data.

## If stock photos are added later (Unsplash, Pexels)
*Summary of the sites' terms as generally published. Re-read the current license pages before using any photo.*

- **Free for commercial use, no payment, and attribution isn't required** (credit is appreciated) on both Unsplash and Pexels.
- **No model or property releases are guaranteed.** People in the photos may not have agreed to appear in health or cancer material. Don't use identifiable faces in ways that imply something about them.
- **Never imply the people shown have cancer**, are patients, or endorse CureBranch. Prefer hands, objects, nature, trees and landscapes, or use clearly labeled illustrations.
- You can't sell unaltered copies, or build a competing photo collection from them.
- Keep a record (URL, photographer, date downloaded) for each photo in `docs/image-credits.md`.

## Better options for a health project
- Commissioned or patient-contributed photos with written consent.
- Illustrations under CC0 or CC BY.
- AI-generated images, clearly labeled as illustrations, and never shown as real patients.
