How CureBranch works

  1. Your records stay with you. You import them into this app. They are saved only in this browser on this device.
  2. Your own AI, your own bill. If you want an AI to read your records, you use your own (Grok, ChatGPT, Claude or any other). CureBranch never runs AI on your data.
  3. A tiny public summary, only if you say so. One switch. When it's on, a short summary (cancer type, markers, treatments, how it went) is shared under a random code like k7x2m9. Never your name, birthday or record numbers.
  4. Only checked records count. Records signed by a hospital or lab count in the tree's numbers. Everything else is shown but not counted. One person, one count.
  5. You decide what happens later. You choose what happens to your summary after you die, and name a person to carry it out.

    Accessibility
    
      
    
      
    We want everyone to be able to use this app, including people with vision, hearing, mobility, or reading differences.
    
      
    Our goal, in plain English
    
      
    We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 Level AA, a widely used checklist for accessible apps and websites. The app has not been formally audited or certified.
    
      
    How to listen
    
      
    Every screen has a Listen button. It uses your browser's free, built-in read-aloud and picks the most natural voice your device has. You can play, pause, stop, and change the speed. Screen readers such as VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS and Narrator also work.
    
      
    How to change the look
    
      
    Open Accessibility from the top corner. You can make text larger (up to 200%), choose a font including OpenDyslexic, change spacing, pick a high-contrast or colorblind-safe theme or your own colors, reduce motion, underline links, and enlarge the focus ring. Your choices stay on this device.
